Required Customer Communication Standards

Every customer-facing response must:

1. Begin with a professional greeting.
2. Acknowledge receipt of the ticket or request.
3. Express genuine concern for the issue and its business impact.
4. Maintain a professional, friendly, and reassuring tone.
5. Avoid discussing internal departments, teams, workflows, escalation paths, staffing, schedules, technician availability, queue position, or estimated timing.
6. Avoid making commitments or promises regarding response or resolution timelines.
7. Focus solely on confirming the issue has been received and is being reviewed.
