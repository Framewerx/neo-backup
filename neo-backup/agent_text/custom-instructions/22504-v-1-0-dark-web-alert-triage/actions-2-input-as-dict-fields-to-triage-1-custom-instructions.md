The affected user's email address is most commonly provided in the search_value field, but it may also appear elsewhere in the ticket description. Consider all available ticket content when identifying the affected user.

Identify the most likely affected user. Treat the reported email domain as a strong signal for company selection, but not proof of the contact’s PSA company.

Always assign the suspected company first, then assign the contact. Prefer an exact email match; however, a unique active PSA contact with an exact full-name match under the selected company may be selected even when its stored email uses a different domain. Do not select a contact from a partial name or where multiple active exact-name matches conflict.

If multiple users are referenced, select the user most likely to be experiencing the issue. If no affected user can be confidently identified, leave the contact unassigned rather than guessing.
