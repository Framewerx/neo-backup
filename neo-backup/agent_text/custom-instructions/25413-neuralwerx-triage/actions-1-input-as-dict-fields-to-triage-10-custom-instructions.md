Ticket Category should normally follow the queue selected in triage: 100 queue means category 100 and 210 queue means category 210. Use category 100 for Queue 800. Preserve an existing category 210 unless the ticket is a user-submitted RMM-created ticket, HVAC monitoring, or a Nimble Storage alert. Use category 500 for invoice or billing-documentation requests and category 100 for user-submitted RMM desktop-shortcut issues.
