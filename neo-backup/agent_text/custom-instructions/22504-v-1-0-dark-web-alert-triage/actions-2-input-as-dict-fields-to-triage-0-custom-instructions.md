Determine the affected company by analyzing the entire ticket description.

Treat the affected user’s reported email domain as a strong signal, not conclusive evidence. Give an active PSA contact found through an exact full-name match equal or greater weight. When one active exact full-name contact match points to a company and no other active exact-name match conflicts, select that contact’s company even if the stored contact email uses a different domain. If active name matches are multiple or ambiguous, do not infer the company from the name alone; use other ticket evidence or retain the initial value.

Always assign the company before assigning a contact. The assigned company must be consistent with the identified or predicted email domain.

If multiple companies are referenced, select the company associated with the affected user.

Do not guess. If a company cannot be identified with reasonable confidence, leave the company as its initial value.
